# JASA — Instructions for Authors

## About the Journal

Journal of the American Statistical Association (JASA) is an international, peer-reviewed journal publishing high-quality, original research. Published quarterly (March, June, September, December). Three main sections:

- **Applications and Case Studies (ACS)**
- **Theory and Methods (T&M)**
- **Reviews**

Plus Book Reviews and Letters to the Editor.

Papers can be submitted to only one section at a time. Previously published or under-review papers will not be considered.

---

## Preparing Your Paper

### General Requirements

- Upload a **cover letter** detailing the significance of your work.
- Order: title page; author footnote; abstract; keywords; article text (tables, figures); acknowledgments; appendices; references.
- **Max 35 pages** including title page, abstract, figures, tables, and references.
- Appendices, proofs, and supporting material in a **separate supplement file** (anonymized for review).
- **Unstructured abstract of 200 words.**
  - First 100 words: succinctly describe motivation and contribution.
  - Remainder: amplify and illustrate, preferably with concrete examples.
  - Do **not** cite references in the abstract.
- **3 to 6 keywords** that do **not** appear in the paper's title.

### Theory and Methods Section

- Publishes articles making original contributions to foundations, theoretical development, and methodology of statistics and probability.
- "Theory and methods" interpreted broadly: includes computations, graphical methods, and traditional mathematical methods.
- Research should be **motivated by a scientific or practical problem** and, ideally, **illustrated by application**.
- Illustration with **real data** is especially welcomed and strongly encouraged.
- Articles should report on **completed research** and **adequately compare methods with existing methods**.
- JASA seeks substantive articles likely to have **broad impact** on theory, methodology, and practice. Narrowly focused contributions may be inappropriate.

### Applications and Case Studies Section

- Publishes original articles demonstrating statistical usage in applications from any research area.
- Traditional ACS: limited to 35 pages (including tables, references, figures).
- Short Communications: 25 pages limit.
- Review papers (ACS): 50 pages limit.
- Must provide:
  - Description of how data were collected/selected.
  - Summary of relevant subject matter background.

## Formatting Requirements

### LaTeX

- Use `article` document class.
- **12 point font** throughout.
- **Fully double-spaced** (26 lines of text per page).
- Font size for tables and captions must also be large enough to read easily.
- Format for **8.5 x 11 inch paper**, one side only.
- Use `natbib.sty` and either `plain.bst` or `apalike.bst` for references.
- Use BibTeX whenever possible.
- Include `.bib` and `.bbl` files upon acceptance.
- Number all pages consecutively.
- Average JASA paper: ~30 manuscript pages. Papers >35 pages burden reviewers and elicit negative reviews.

### Review Process

- **Double-anonymous** peer review.
- Submit **two files**: one anonymous (no author names/institutions), one with identification.
- Authors are solely responsible for removing identity clues, including references to unpublished work and funding sources.
- Supporting manuscripts should be submitted anonymously as well.

### Figures and Tables

- Numbered consecutively with arabic numerals.
- Clearly titled and labeled.
- Appear in the body of the manuscript **as soon as possible after first reference** (not in separate files or at the end).
- Color: reproduced in color online at no cost. Print color: $400/figure (first 4), $75/figure (5+).
- **Figure captions and text must not refer specifically to any color.** E.g., write "the dashed line shows..." not "the blue line shows..."
- Same figure must serve for both color and black-and-white versions.
- Art files preferred: EPS, PS; also acceptable: PDF, TIFF, JPEG, BMP.
- Minimum 300 dpi for color, 600 dpi for grayscale, 1200 dpi for line art.

### Supplementary Materials

- Encouraged to facilitate review and support results.
- Must be **anonymous**.
- May contain technical derivations, proofs, code, data.
- Posted online at JASA website upon acceptance.

### Reproducibility (as of Sept 1, 2021)

- Both ACS and T&M undergo **reproducibility review**.
- If invited for revision: complete **Author Contributions Checklist (ACC)**.
- Submit ACC form as separate PDF.
- Submit reproducibility materials as a **single zip file** (suggested name: `reproducibility_materials.zip`).
- Remove identifying author information from code/data to extent possible.
- Published papers include link to reviewed reproducibility materials on Figshare.

## Style Guidelines

- Follow the **ASA Style Guide**.
- **American spelling** throughout.
- Double quotation marks (except quotation within quotation).
- Long quotations: indented, no quotation marks.
- **Footnotes**: use rarely, no mathematical expressions.
- **Avoid abbreviations** as much as possible.
- Exponentials: write `exp()`.
- Fractions in text: use solidus, e.g., `(w+x)/(y+z)`.
- No overbars extending over more than one character; no underbars.
- **Boldface** for vectors and matrices.
- Avoid ambiguous characters (e.g., lowercase "l" vs "1").
- References cited in text with authors' names and dates.
- Discourage inessential unpublished or obscure references.

## Submission

- Submit via Taylor & Francis Submission Portal.
- LaTeX: convert to PDF for upload as manuscript file. Also upload LaTeX source as single zip.
- Papers screened for plagiarism via Crossref.
- No submission fees, publication fees, or page charges.
- Revised manuscripts resubmitted >1 year after last editor action may be treated as new submissions.
